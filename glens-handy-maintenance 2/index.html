About

Owner-operated, detail-obsessed.

Glen Shelton runs Glen's Handy Maintenance out of Pine Grove, taking on the kind of jobs that need real craftsmanship — not a quick patch. That means stripping and refinishing original hardwood floors board by board, building custom vanities and fixtures from raw lumber, and keeping older log cabins and mountain properties standing strong for another generation.

Every job is treated like it's Glen's own home: measured twice, matched to the existing character of the house, and finished clean. No subcontractors passing through — just one person who shows up and gets it right.

  • Serving all of Amador County, CA
  • Log cabins & rustic mountain homes a specialty
  • Interior & exterior work, one job at a time
  • Straightforward quotes, no middlemen
